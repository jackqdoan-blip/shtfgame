How the nutrition estimate works and its limitations

Built-in foods use generic category-based estimates per 100 g, converted using an approximate edible weight per entered unit. Brand labels, recipes, draining, fortification, cooking losses and package sizes can change actual values significantly. Use the editable grams-per-unit and custom-food nutrition fields to improve the estimate. Reference targets are simplified adult 19–50 planning values; children, pregnancy, breastfeeding, older adults and medical conditions need different targets. A low modelled percentage does not diagnose a deficiency, and a high score does not prove a diet is safe or complete.

For better exact product data, use the Australian Food Composition Database or AUSNUT 2023, and check reference values at Eat for Health.
